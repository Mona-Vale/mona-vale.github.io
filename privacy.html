Mona Vale Studio: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026.

What we collect

When you connect a TikTok account, Mona Vale Studio receives through TikTok's official APIs: your account's open ID and basic profile information, access tokens that let us send drafts to your TikTok inbox, and public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) for videos you publish from those drafts.

How we use it

Only to send drafts you have scheduled to your own inbox and to show you how those videos perform. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Encrypted access tokens and video statistics are stored on Cloudflare infrastructure. Draft videos are hosted on this site only until TikTok has retrieved them.

Retention and deletion

Revoking access in TikTok stops all data collection. To have all stored data deleted, contact the contact address listed on this site; we delete it within 30 days.

Children

Mona Vale Studio is not intended for anyone under 18.
